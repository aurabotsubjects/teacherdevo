# Devotions 2 and 3: Image Prompts (for ChatGPT)

**Devotions:** 2 (Fear) and 3 (Discontentment). Devotion 2's images are already in the app.

The app is one `index.html` plus a `data/` folder. Each devotion has its own data file (text and image info), and an optional images file with the pictures embedded:

```
index.html
data/
  devotions.js                (the list shown in the menus; add a line per new devotion)
  devotion-1.js  devotion-1-images.js
  devotion-2.js  devotion-2-images.js
  devotion-3.js               (no images file yet)
images/                       (optional: only for devotions without an images file)
  g3-01-title.jpg  g3-02-scripture.jpg  g3-03-seat.jpg  g3-04-crab.jpg
  g3-05-grass.jpg  g3-06-joy.jpg  g3-07-closing.jpg
```

Use the **exact file names** under each prompt below. Send the finished images to me and I'll embed them as `data/devotion-3-images.js`, or drop the files into an `images/` folder next to `index.html` and the app will load them by name. Until an image exists, the slide shows the animated indigo/mint gradient, so nothing looks broken.

### Style block: paste at the start of every prompt

> Premium editorial duotone illustration. Strict colour palette only: deep onyx black (#01010D), electric indigo (#6331FB) and tropical mint (#88F9C0), with white highlights. Cinematic rim lighting, soft indigo glow, subtle film grain, high contrast, modern magazine-cover feel. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks anywhere in the image.

Sizes: **16:9 = 1920×1080** (title, scripture, closing). **1:1 = 1200×1200** (the four teaching panels).

### Tips
- If ChatGPT adds writing or numbers, reply: "Regenerate with absolutely no text or numerals."
- If the colours drift, reply: "Use only these three colours: #01010D, #6331FB and #88F9C0."
- Export as JPG (about 85% quality). Backgrounds under 500 KB and panels under 300 KB keep the page fast.
- Faces are deliberately avoided throughout: silhouettes keep the look consistent.

## Content notes
- **Source use:** book illustrations are paraphrased briefly (coatrack, "Beware of the dog" puppy, swimming lesson, Austrian false retreat; airplane seat and crab, grizzly and skunk, forty moves, beggar) and credited by the series line to Kent Crockett's *Slaying Your Giants*. No long quotes.
- **Scripture:** KJV throughout. Joshua 1:1–9 and Philippians 4:10–13 are meant to be read aloud in full before the key verse.
- **Teaching slide labels:** the four middle slides are labelled "Teaching 1 of 4" and so on (Devotion 1 used "Fact 1 of 4", which suited that chapter).
