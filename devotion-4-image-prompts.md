# Devotion 4: Doubt (Matthew 11:2–6) · Image Prompts (for ChatGPT)

Save each image with the **exact file name** below. Send the finished images back to me and I'll embed them as `data/devotion-4-images.js`, or drop them in an `images/` folder next to `index.html` and the app loads them by name. Until an image exists, the slide shows the animated indigo/mint gradient, so nothing looks broken.

Sizes: **16:9 = 1920×1080** (title, scripture, closing). **1:1 = 1200×1200** (the four teaching panels).

### Style block: paste at the start of every prompt

> Premium editorial duotone illustration. Strict colour palette only: deep onyx black (#01010D), electric indigo (#6331FB) and tropical mint (#88F9C0), with white highlights. Cinematic rim lighting, soft indigo glow, subtle film grain, high contrast, modern magazine-cover feel. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks anywhere in the image.

Faces are deliberately avoided: silhouettes keep the look consistent and avoid portraits of biblical figures.
